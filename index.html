What is an Election?

Understanding democracy in very simple words

🤝

Choosing Your Representatives

An election is like choosing a captain for your cricket team or a monitor for your class. As citizens, we choose leaders to make decisions about roads, schools, hospitals, and laws for all of us.

⚖️

Every Single Vote is Equal

Whether someone is rich or poor, young or old, a farmer or a doctor — everyone gets exactly one vote, and every vote has the exact same value.

🔒

Your Vote is 100% Secret

Inside the voting compartment, nobody can see which button you press. No leader, no family member, and no police officer can ever know who you voted for.

📢

Your Voice in the Government

When you vote, you hold leaders accountable. Good leaders are encouraged to work hard, and people can choose new leaders if they are not satisfied.

Who Can Vote in India?

3 simple rules determine if you can cast your vote

🎂

1. Age 18 or Older

You must be at least 18 years old on the qualifying date (January 1st, April 1st, July 1st, or October 1st).

🇮🇳

2. Indian Citizen

You must be a citizen of India and ordinarily residing in your constituency.

📋

3. Name in the Voter List

Most important! Your name MUST be listed in the official Electoral Roll (Voter List) of your area.

The Election Process in 5 Simple Steps

From voter registration to seeing results on television

Step 1

Voter Registration

You register as a voter using Form 6. Election officials verify your details and assign you a local Polling Booth.

Step 2

Know Your Candidates

Learn about the candidates standing in your area. Look at their past work, qualifications, and plans. You can use the official KYC (Know Your Candidate) app.

Step 3

Locate Your Polling Station

A few days before voting, find out your designated booth (often a local government school or community center near your home).

Step 4

Cast Your Secret Vote

Visit the booth on voting day between 7:00 AM and 6:00 PM. Get your finger inked, step into the voting compartment, and press your choice on the EVM.

Step 5

Counting & Results

On counting day, all sealed EVMs from every booth are opened in front of candidate representatives. The candidate with the highest votes wins!

What to Do Before Voting Day

A quick preparation guide so your voting day goes smoothly without stress

🔍

1. Search Your Name in Voter List

Never go directly without checking. Visit voters.eci.gov.in or SMS your EPIC number to 1950 to confirm your name and serial number.

🪪

2. Keep Your Photo ID Ready

Keep your Voter ID (EPIC), Aadhaar card, or Driving License ready in your purse or pocket the night before.

📍

3. Note Down Your Polling Booth Address

Check where your booth is located and plan your travel. Early morning (7:00 AM - 9:30 AM) usually has shorter queues.

📵

4. No Smartphones in Voting Compartment

Taking photos or videos of the EVM is strictly illegal to protect vote secrecy. Leave mobile phones at home or outside the booth.

What Happens Inside the Polling Station?

Step-by-step walkthrough so you know exactly what to expect inside

👮‍♂️

1. Security & Queue

Friendly police or security guards direct voters into separate queues for senior citizens, women, and men. Wait patiently for your turn.

📇

2. First Polling Officer (Identification)

Show your ID. The officer checks your name in the voter list, announces your name and serial number loudly so party agents can verify.

🖊️

3. Second Polling Officer (Ink & Signature)

The officer applies indelible blue-black ink on your left index finger, takes your signature or thumbprint in the register (Form 17A), and gives you a small voter slip.

🖲️

4. Third Polling Officer (EVM Activation)

Give the slip to the third officer. They inspect your inked finger and press the 'BALLOT' button on the Control Unit to activate the voting machine for you.

🗳️

5. The Voting Compartment (Your Secret Vote)

Walk alone into the enclosed voting booth. On the Ballot Unit, find the name and symbol of your choice. Press the BLUE button next to it.

🔔

6. Beep & VVPAT Slip Confirmation

A red light glows next to your button and a loud 'BEEP' sounds. Look at the VVPAT machine's transparent window: a paper slip showing your candidate's serial number, name, and symbol is visible for 7 seconds before dropping into the sealed box!

✌️

7. Proudly Exit With Inked Finger

Your vote is cast! You can now walk out proudly, show off your inked finger, and inspire family and neighbors to vote.

Easy-to-Follow Election Timeline

From the first announcement to the final victory celebration

1

1. Announcement & Model Code of Conduct

Election Commission announces election dates. The Model Code of Conduct comes into force immediately to prevent misuse of government machinery.

2

2. Filing Nominations

Candidates file nomination papers with details of wealth, education, and criminal records (Form 26).

3

3. Scrutiny & Final List

Officials verify papers. Candidates who wish to withdraw may do so. The final list of contesting candidates and symbols is published.

4

4. Campaigning Period

Candidates hold rallies, distribute manifestos, and meet citizens to explain their vision.

5

5. 48-Hour Silence Period

48 hours before polling ends, all public campaigning, rallies, and loudspeakers must stop so voters can think calmly without noise.

6

6. Polling Day (Voting Day)

Citizens cast their votes from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. EVMs are sealed in front of representatives.

7

7. Counting & Results Day

Sealed EVMs are opened at counting centers under tight security. Results are announced round by round.
